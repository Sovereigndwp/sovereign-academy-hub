Institutional Programs

Financial education programs for the people you serve.

The Sovereign Academy works with employers, workforce organizations, schools, correctional and reentry programs, municipalities, and community partners. Choose the educational focus that fits your organization — practical financial education, Bitcoin-specific education, or both.

Programs typically begin with a free 45-minute strategy consultation. Delivery available in English and Spanish.

Two Program Tracks

Start with everyday finance, add Bitcoin when it fits.

Many organizations begin with financial education foundations and add Bitcoin-specific content as a later phase. Others need Bitcoin education immediately. Both tracks share the same approach: real decisions, practice activities, and facilitator support.
