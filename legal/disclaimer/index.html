9. Bitcoin-specific risks

Our Bitcoin content teaches how the system works, including its risks. Anyone acting in the Bitcoin economy should understand at minimum:

  • Volatility. Bitcoin's price can change rapidly and severely; amounts shown in fiat terms in our materials can be stale the moment they are published.
  • Irreversibility. Bitcoin transactions cannot be reversed, and there is no customer-service layer; a mistaken or fraudulent transaction is usually unrecoverable.
  • Key loss and custody. Whoever controls the keys controls the bitcoin. Losing keys, seed phrases, or passphrases usually means permanent loss; custody choices (self-custody, collaborative custody, third-party custody) trade off different risks, and our materials explain those tradeoffs without choosing for you.
  • Scams and coercion. The ecosystem attracts fraud — fake support, phishing, "recovery services," extortion. No legitimate educator, including us, will ever ask for your seed phrase.
  • Protocol and network risk. Software bugs, forks, fee-market changes, and mining dynamics can affect usability and value.
  • Regulatory and tax risk. The legal and tax treatment of Bitcoin varies by jurisdiction and changes; see §11.

The Sovereign Academy LLC does not sell, broker, exchange, custody, or transmit Bitcoin or any other asset, and does not operate a money-services business.
